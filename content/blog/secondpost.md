The idea of print on demand captured my imagination.

## 2/2/2026

The machine sits lifeless until. An order from far away is passed through a web. A screen illuminates, gears turn, ink flows, pages stack and attach. In the back a date the only evidence of the recent process. The book exists in the machine until it is called upon to create a physical version. Thousands of books packed into silicon wafers. Waiting on the signal to become material.
